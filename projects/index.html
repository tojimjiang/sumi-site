Side quests & small experiments

Projects

Nice to see you taking a peek. Some of these are just me playing around; others solve a quality-of-life problem in a technology or service I use every day. A few carefully placed needles can do a lot.

01

Breaking stuff

Tools that some other parties might not want to exist. The endpoints are there for the taking. 😈

dreamtA ↗

Live

A dreamy transit client for NYC subways. Outrageously lightweight and powerful — when the MTA isn’t lying about where the trains are.

02

Graveyard 🪦

Good ideas, expired hosting, and things that are no longer maintained.

spearfish ↗

Inactive

A little tool for finding better prices at a big-box retailer by poking at a flaw in its pricing engine. Formerly hosted on Glitch; no longer active.

unsupress ↗

Unmaintained

A Chrome extension that pulls back the curtain on the real quantity a seller has listed — and is committed to selling — on a particular e-commerce platform.

03

“Ancient” UI stuff

Earlier, self-contained projects. Mostly a showcase of what responsive JavaScript can do — and a little bit of silliness.

Word Based Clock ↗

Browser toy

A word clock in your browser, synchronized to local time with a little JavaScript magic. Currently dark mode only.

Dot Matrix LED Emulator ↗

Browser toy

Emulate a dot-matrix LED display in full RGB. Make custom colors and designs, or turn text into a quick display message.

Color Mini-Canvas ↗

Browser toy

A tiny, ephemeral digital canvas for trying out colors and designs without turning your home into a paint-splattered mess.

Candy Matching ↗

Browser toy

Definitely not a Candy Crush ripoff. More of a flip-card game, with candy as the medium. It can get loud; mute controls are available on the page, and scores can be saved locally.

Definitely Not Evil Dice Roller ↗

Browser toy

A dice game for the comfort of your own home, without losing actual money to a definitely-not-rigged game. (It’s rigged.)
